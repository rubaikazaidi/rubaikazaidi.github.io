About me

I started programming and robotics at eight and have been building ever since. I’m drawn to machine learning and applied AI, especially where they solve real problems. AquaSmart, my irrigation system for the Earth Prize, uses soil moisture and rainfall sensors to cut water waste, and at SAAI I built a sentiment-classification pipeline for IPL text. I work in Python and Java, and I’m strengthening my machine learning fundamentals through experiments, evaluation and deployment.

Shooting runs alongside this. At the 2025 National Shooting Championship I raised my 10m air rifle score by 13.6 points over the year before. Training taught me that progress comes from small, repeatable habits, measured honestly. I want to bring that approach to Computer Science & Engineering, and my next project will turn my own training data into charts and models.

Outside class I’m a self-taught guitarist. In my free time I learn new tunes and experiment with them until they sound right. I also read widely, because books and literature show me perspectives, ideas and stories far beyond my coursework.
