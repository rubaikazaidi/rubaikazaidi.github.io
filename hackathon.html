From One Idea to Four Functional Apps

A look back at organising the inaugural hackathon at Avalon Heights International School, where ideas turned into applications and curiosity turned into creation.

By Rubaika Zaidi • 2 min read • Hackathon

Recently, I had the opportunity to be part of the organising committee for the inaugural hackathon at Avalon Heights International School.

1 great idea, 2 weeks of planning, 3 days of training, and 4 teams building 4 functional applications!

On Saturday morning, a group of curious, bright-eyed students from Grades 9 to 11 came together to exchange ideas, raise questions, experiment and collaborate. As the day progressed, their initial ideas evolved into thoughtful design choices, creative solutions and functional applications.

By evening, as the teams took the stage to present their work, it was incredibly rewarding to see what a little guidance, encouragement and the freedom to experiment could inspire.

It was a reminder that sometimes, all it takes to turn an idea into something tangible is the right environment and an opportunity to build.

I'm grateful to Indu Ma'am for entrusting us with this opportunity and to Huma for her constant guidance and support throughout the event.

Here's to more ideas, more experimentation, and many more opportunities to build!
