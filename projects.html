Learning experience

Classical machine learning on IPL sentiment

Comparing five classical machine learning models revealed how different vectorisation and classification techniques handle informal, short-form IPL tweets. CountVectorizer with Multinomial Naive Bayes performed best, while character-level TF-IDF proved useful for handling abbreviations and spelling variations. Next, I'd experiment with larger datasets and transformer-based models such as BERT.
