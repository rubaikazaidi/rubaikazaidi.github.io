Learning experience

Three positions, one routine

Transitioning from 10m Air Rifle to 50m Smallbore challenged everything I thought I knew about shooting. Mastering Prone, Standing, and Kneeling required me to rethink my technique, build physical resilience, and adapt to a completely different discipline. If there is one thing I have learned, it is that consistency beats perfection; trust your process, especially on the difficult days.
